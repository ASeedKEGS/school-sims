What actually happens
  1. More CO₂ in the air
  2. More outgoing IR absorbed & re-radiated down — the blanket
  3. Surface warms
  4. Ice & snow melt
  5. Albedo falls — darker surface reflects less
  6. More sunlight absorbed → extra warming feedback

Albedo change is a result of warming that amplifies it — not the step between CO₂ and temperature.

Why CO₂ acts as a blanket

Visible sunlight — the wrong frequency for CO₂, so it passes straight through the molecules.

Infrared from the warm ground matches CO₂'s vibrations: it is absorbed, the molecule bends & stretches, then re-emits IR in a random direction — often back down.

More CO₂ → more IR intercepted → a bigger share sent back to Earth instead of escaping to space.
